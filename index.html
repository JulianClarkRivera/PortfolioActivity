Cafenalé

Point of Sale (POS) Emulator

Led the development of a Java-based Point-of-Sale emulator. Users can select drinks (with size and quantity) and pastries, view their cart, and enter the tendered cash. It also includes a PWD/Senior Citizen discount prompt and automatic calculation of cost and change. It is designed to emulate real POS transactions within an IDE terminal, with a smooth and user-friendly program flow.
